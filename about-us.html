About Us

At Plank and Slate, we are dedicated to providing top-notch flooring solutions that combine quality, durability, and aesthetic appeal. With years of experience in the industry, our team of experts specializes in the installation, restoration, and maintenance of Luxury Vinyl Plank, Luxury Vinyl Tile, Slate, and Tile flooring.

We pride ourselves on our commitment to customer satisfaction, ensuring that every project is completed to the highest standards. Whether you're looking to upgrade your home's flooring or need professional advice for a commercial space, Plank and Slate is here to help.

Our mission is to transform spaces with beautiful flooring options that meet the unique needs of each client. We work closely with you from consultation to completion, ensuring a seamless experience and exceptional results.

With years of experience in the flooring industry, Plank and Slate is dedicated to providing top-tier LVP and LVT installations. Our team utilizes advanced moisture mitigation methods to prevent future moisture-related problems, ensuring the longevity and integrity of your floors.

Choose Plank and Slate for your next flooring project and experience the difference that quality craftsmanship and personalized service can make.
