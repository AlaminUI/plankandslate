Why is moisture protection critical for Holiday FL flooring?

Holiday sits directly on Florida's Gulf Coast just north of Clearwater with constant salt air exposure and year-round humidity levels of 80%+. The proximity to the Anclote River and Gulf waters creates extreme moisture challenges. Without proper moisture protection, floors in Holiday homes face warping, buckling, mold growth, and costly damage within 2-3 years. Our proprietary moisture barrier system creates an impermeable layer between your subfloor and new flooring, preventing moisture from below and protecting against the high humidity levels common in Holiday's coastal location.

For Holiday waterfront properties along the Anclote River and pool homes throughout the area, we recommend 100% waterproof luxury vinyl plank (LVP) or luxury vinyl tile (LVT).
